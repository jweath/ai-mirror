Sources and update timing

Project pins are entered by the instructor with a source link. The label distinguishes a student statement from an instructor note. Captured Slack replies keep each partner’s words separate; private muddy points, journals, and digests stay outside this board.

The page checks saved records every 30 seconds while visible. GitHub collection runs about every six hours when the course and collection are active, or when you choose Collect GitHub. The feed covers a rolling 14 days. A page refresh does not fetch new GitHub history. Collection failures and stale records stay visible. GitHub activity does not establish learning or effort.

Product links open the supplied build. They do not certify that the product works. This view is private; a course-site link does not publish its records.
